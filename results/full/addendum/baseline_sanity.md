## General note on why our absolute numbers would differ from any published baseline, even where methods nominally match

Window/session definition (HDFS: per-block-id; BGL: fixed 100-line windows -- both specific, documented choices, `PROTOCOL.md` Section 5.1), the RANDOM-vs-CHRONO split itself (this entire project's subject), validation-chosen vs. test-tuned thresholds (`F1_val` vs `F1_oracle`, Section 5.4), the exact Drain3 parser configuration (similarity 0.4, depth 4, our own masking rules), and training-budget caps for the trainable arms (DeepLog's 100k-window/5-epoch cap; OCSVM's 50k-session subsample) are all specific choices documented in `PROTOCOL.md` that any other paper's numbers would need to match exactly to be directly comparable -- which published baselines, in general, do not.
